# Billing System Migration: What's Changing

## Overview

Starting this quarter, we are migrating every customer's billing profile from the
legacy ledger service to the new unified billing platform. This migration affects
how invoices are generated, how payment methods are stored, and how usage-based
charges are calculated. The migration is being rolled out account-by-account over
six weeks rather than all at once, so you may see the new billing experience before
or after other customers in your organization.

No action is required from most customers. Your existing invoices, payment history,
and saved cards will be copied over automatically. You will not be double-charged
during the transition, and any invoice generated in the final week of the old system
will still be honored under its original terms.

## Timeline

The migration runs in four phases:

1. **Phase 1 (Weeks 1-2):** Read-only shadow copy. Your data is mirrored into the new
   platform but the old ledger remains the system of record. You will not notice any
   change yet.
2. **Phase 2 (Weeks 3-4):** Cutover. Your account is switched to the new platform as
   the system of record. Invoices from this point forward are generated by the new
   platform.
3. **Phase 3 (Week 5):** Verification. Support runs automated reconciliation between
   the old and new ledgers to catch discrepancies.
4. **Phase 4 (Week 6):** Legacy decommission. The old ledger becomes read-only and is
   retained for 90 days for audit purposes only.

Most support tickets are expected during Phase 2, when accounts actually cut over.

## What You Need To Do

Before your account's cutover date, we recommend the following:

- Confirm your default payment method is current, since the new platform re-validates
  card details on first use after cutover.
- Export any historical invoices you need for your own records if you rely on
  third-party accounting tools that only read from the legacy export format.
- If you use the billing API directly, review the new API version notes, since a small
  number of field names changed (for example `invoice_total` is now `total_amount`).
- Notify any team members who receive billing notification emails, since notification
  sender addresses are changing as part of the migration.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Will my subscription price change?** No. Pricing is unaffected by this migration;
only the underlying system that tracks and bills for it is changing.

**What happens if something goes wrong during my cutover?** Support can manually roll
an account back to the legacy ledger during Phase 2 if a critical issue is found. This
is a manual process and should be requested through a support ticket, not attempted by
the customer.

**Where do I check my cutover phase?** Your account settings page shows a "Billing
Platform" field that reads either "Legacy" or "Unified" depending on your current
phase.
